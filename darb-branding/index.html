01

Foundation

One console for a multi-platform delivery fleet in Kuwait. Four feeds, one honest picture.

Positioning

A system of record, not a tracker.

Audience

Ops managers, accountants, couriers.

Character

Warm, exact, unhurried. Calm over neon.

The three commitments

CommitmentWhat it means in practice
Legible under pressure Status readable at a glance. Shape and colour, never a number alone.
Bilingual by default Every screen mirrors, every value formats in ar-KW.
Neutral to platforms Platform colour marks a row's origin, never Darb's voice.
02

Logo

A road receding to a horizon, sun on the line. Lowercase wordmark, one full stop for a flourish.

v1.0 recolours the mark to navy and sand, with a yellow dot. The sun and full stop are #F5C518, which holds at roughly 9:1 on navy‑deep, where navy on navy would vanish.

Primary lockup

Mark + wordmark + descriptor. Default above 200px.

Primary: navy-deep on sand · minimum width 200px
Reversed: on navy-deep, sand wordmark
One-colour: engraving, fax, embroidery, stamps

Construction & clear space

The mark diameter is the unit, D. Everything derives from it.

MeasureValueNote
Mark diameterDThe unit. Everything below is a fraction of it.
Clear space, all sides0.5 DNothing enters this zone: not type, not rules, not a card edge.
Mark → wordmark gap0.30 DOptical, measured to the d stem, not the bounding box.
Wordmark cap height0.54 DBaseline aligns to the mark's optical centre.
Descriptor size0.13 DTracking +0.15em, weight 600, colour #4A4744.
Full-stop diameter0.16 DAlways #F5C518, the yellow dot; navy on the one-colour mark.

Minimum sizes

LockupScreenPrintBelow that, use
Primary, with descriptor200 px45 mmCompact lockup
Compact, no descriptor104 px24 mmMark only
Mark only24 px8 mmNothing. Do not go smaller.

Below 24px, favicon/app icon use a simplified mark: solid road, no dashes, sun kept.

Rules

Do
  • Place the mark on sand, white, or navy‑deep.
  • Scale the whole lockup as one locked group.
  • Use the reversed lockup on any ground darker than #4A4744.
  • Keep the descriptor only where it is legible at 11px or larger.
Don't
  • Recolour the mark to a platform colour to "match" a section.
  • Set the wordmark in title case, all caps, or any face other than the brand sans.
  • Add a gradient, bevel, outer glow, or drop shadow to the mark.
  • Stretch, rotate, or reflow the lockup vertically without the approved stacked variant.
  • Place the lockup over a photograph without a navy‑deep veil beneath it.

Weights

Almarai ships four weights and no italic. That is fewer than most systems assume, and the constraint is worth keeping: two weights do all the work, and emphasis never relies on a slant.

WeightRoleNotes
300LightLarge Arabic display only, above 36px.
400RegularBody, cells, headings, form values. The default.
700BoldTitles, labels, buttons, badges. The only accent weight.
800ExtraboldWordmark and cover slides only.
No 500 or 600. Ask for 400 or 700 directly. Never synthetic bold or oblique, it breaks Arabic joins.

05

Voice & tone

Darb reports. It does not cheer, apologise, or hedge.

Plain

Say the thing people say out loud. A courier is offline, not in a non-transmitting state.

Exact

Numbers over adjectives. "3,232 m from the customer", not "far from the customer".

Even

A violation and a payout get the same register. No exclamation marks anywhere in the product.

Instead ofWrite
Oops! Something went wrong 😕Couldn't save the shift. Check the courier still has an active contract, then try again.
Successfully processed!Settled. 42 orders, KD 128.400.
Driver has violated policyDropped off 3,232 m from the customer at 14:02.
Configure webhook endpointChoose where Darb sends notifications.
Are you sure you want to proceed?Suspend Ahmed Al-Rashidi? He'll be removed from today's shift.

Buttons say what happens

Verb + object, confirmation echoes in past tense. Publish → Published. Never Submit, OK, Done.

Four values, not six. rounded-sm / rounded-lg are legacy only.

Keyboard focus. 3px navy at 18%, never a browser default outline.

Shadows are tinted rgba(15,31,24,·), a green-black rather than neutral black. On sand, a pure black shadow reads grey and dirty.

Motion confirms an action or reveals structure. Nothing loops, nothing draws attention to itself, and everything collapses to 0ms under prefers-reduced-motion.

Components

Live specimens. These are rendered from the same values the product uses, so what you see is what a correctly-built component looks like.

Buttons

40px tall, fully pilled, 14px medium. Three variants and no more.

Primary · Secondary · Disabled (opacity .5, no press transform)
ghost: navy grounds only
Do
  • One primary per view. If two things compete, one of them is secondary.
  • Press feedback is scale(.98), not a colour flash.
  • Destructive actions use secondary styling plus a red-text confirm in the dialog.
Don't
  • Invent a red or amber filled button. Semantic colour never fills a control.
  • Mix rounded-xl and rounded-pill buttons in one view.
  • Ship a disabled button with no explanation of what would enable it.

Arabic & RTL

Kuwait runs in both languages, often in one sentence. Arabic is structural, not a feature flag.

English, LTR
Courier performance

42 orders completed · 7h 12m online

Arabic, RTL
أداء المندوبين

٤٢ طلب مكتمل · ٧ س ١٢ د متصل

RuleDetail
One face, both scriptsAlmarai covers Arabic and Latin, so nothing swaps at the language boundary. Under [dir="rtl"] or [lang="ar"] only the letter-spacing resets to 0, because negative tracking mangles Arabic joins.
Logical properties onlyms-* / me-* / ps-* / pe-* / text-start / text-end. Physical left and right will not mirror.
Icons mirror selectivelyDirectional glyphs (chevrons, arrows, back/forward) flip via <DirectionalIcon>. Clocks, logos, maps, media controls and platform marks never flip.
Numbers stay LatinMoney, IDs, plates and phone numbers render in Latin digits in both locales. Ops staff cross-reference platform exports that only ever use Latin figures.
Currency flips positionKD 128.400 becomes 128.400 د.ك, so the symbol trails the value in Arabic.
Dates use ar-KWGregorian calendar, Intl formatting, Arabic month names. Never a hand-built date string.
Mixed content is isolatedA Latin name inside Arabic text needs <bdi>, or punctuation will jump to the wrong end of the line.

Beyond contrast

Required
  • Focus is always visible: 3px navy ring at 18%, offset 2px. Never outline:none alone.
  • Touch targets ≥ 44 × 44px on mobile, ≥ 32px on desktop pointers.
  • Status carries a dot or icon as well as colour, never colour alone.
  • Every icon-only control has an aria-label.
  • Body text never below 12px; 14px is the practical floor for anything read repeatedly.
  • All motion collapses under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.
Watch for
  • Muted grey used for real content instead of placeholders. It fails at 2.41:1.
  • Disabled controls at opacity-50 dropping below 3:1. Add helper text.
  • Map pins as the only carrier of state; the list must say the same thing in words.
  • Toasts that vanish before an eight-second read.

Known drift

Where the codebase currently contradicts the rules above. Ordered by user visibility.

High
Two disagreeing platform palettes
constants/platforms.ts (Americana #E63946) contradicts tailwind.config.ts (#0066FF). Consolidate on config values.
High
Three separate badge palettes
StatusBadge, PlatformBadge and OrderStatusBadge.tsx disagree; the last bypasses tokens with raw blue-50 / green-50. One badge should own all three.
High
RTL alignment will not mirror
Physical text-left / text-right outnumber logical by ~414 to 175. Each stays put when the page flips to Arabic. Highest-value sweep.
Medium
The logo does not match the product
public/logo.png is still navy/amber in SidebarV2.tsx:192. Replace with the navy recolour as SVG.
Medium
The product runs Almarai in Arabic only
Arabic ships Almarai self-hosted. Latin still sets SF Pro Display from fonts.cdnfonts.com on every load. Switch the sans / display stacks to Almarai and drop the last @import.
Medium
No shared Button component
No components/ui/. 36 files hand-roll buttons across rounded-xl / rounded-pill and mixed disabled opacities.
Medium
Numeric columns are not tabular by default
DataTable has no number-alignment convention; only 41 files use tabular-nums. Digits wobble between rows.
Low
Shadow and radius drift
shadow-sm outnumbers shadow-soft 205 to 53; six radius values against the sanctioned four.
Low
Off-grid icon sizes
Lucide icons ship at 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 and 18px. Standardise on 14 and 16.
